Adding, editing, or removing bookable providers is not available in this pilot yet. Customers can continue booking with the provider created during setup.

For this pilot: Use the booking link after setup to confirm your current provider is visible to customers.

Only the shop owner can add team members during the pilot. A barber account cannot add team members.

Additional provider accounts need their own sign-in and hours before they can accept bookings. That workflow will be added separately.
